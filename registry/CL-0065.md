Mechanism (do not reuse): Archimedes displacement: equal mass, lower density (lead) means larger volume and more water displaced.
Script (EN): Three gold bars, the same weight. One is a fraud, and water will expose it. A courier delivers three bars, one kilo each. One may be lead, painted gold. The detective lowers each bar into a tank of water. Ines's bar raises the water fifty-two milliliters. Joel's bar raises the water fifty-two milliliters. Kurt's bar raises the water eighty-eight milliliters. Lead takes up more space than gold for the same weight. Who delivered the fake? Five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Kurt.
