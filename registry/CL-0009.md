Question: Who drowned Leo?
Clues: 1) Pond weed in Leo's lungs and hair; nothing grows in a chlorinated pool | 2) It hasn't rained in weeks | 3) Ben's boots are drying in front of the library fire though he 'never left'
False lead: Ray, the pool cleaner, is soaking wet.
Solution: Ben, the brother
Explanation: Pond weed in Leo's lungs means he drowned in the pond, not the chlorinated pool, so the body was moved. Ben claims he never left the library, yet his boots are drying by the fire on a night it never rained — the only way to get them wet was the pond. Ray is wet from diving into the pool (false lead).
Mechanism (do not reuse): Freshwater weed in the lungs proves a drowned body was moved from a pond to a chlorinated pool; wet boots on a dry night expose the mover.
