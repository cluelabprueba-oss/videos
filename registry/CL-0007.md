# CL-0007 — The Silent Dog
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-15 (batch run 2026-11-14 UTC) · Category: Think like a detective · Language: English
Setting: Moreau farmhouse. The cash box stolen between 1 and 3 AM.
Suspects:
- THE COURIER (Stranger): Seen near the gate at midnight. Nobody knows him.
- JONAS (New tenant): Moved in yesterday. Bruno barked at him all morning.
- FELIX (Nephew): Walks Bruno every single day.
Clues:
1) Bruno barks at anyone he doesn't know
2) The neighbor was awake all night: total silence
3) The thief walked right past Bruno's kennel
Solution: Felix. Bruno barks at every stranger, even at the new tenant. That night he stayed silent while the thief walked past his kennel. So the thief was someone Bruno knew well: the nephew who walks him every day.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Guard dog's silence proves the intruder was familiar to the dog.
