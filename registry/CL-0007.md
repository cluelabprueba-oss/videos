Question: Is Daniel telling the truth about when he arrived?
Clues: 1) Files switched at 11:30; Daniel claims he walked in at 11:53 and poured a whisky with ice | 2) His glass: ice completely melted, glass room temperature, no condensation | 3) The ice bucket beside it is still full of solid cubes
False lead: The waitress's fingerprints are on the safe door.
Solution: No — Daniel had been in the lounge long before 11:30; he switched the files.
Explanation: Ice that was poured two minutes earlier cannot be completely melted, with a warm glass and no condensation, while the ice bucket next to it is still full of solid cubes. The drink had been sitting for a long time, so Daniel was in the lounge at 11:30. The waitress's prints are from polishing the safe at 10 (false lead).
Mechanism (do not reuse): Fully melted ice in a 'fresh' drink, compared to solid ice in the bucket, dates a person's presence.

No. Ice poured two minutes ago can't vanish, not when the cubes in the bucket are still solid.
That drink had sat there for a long time. Daniel was in the lounge at eleven thirty. The waitress just did her job.
