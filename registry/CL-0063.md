# CL-0063 — The Wednesday Postmark

Created: 2026-11-06 · Category: Hidden clue · Language: English
Suspects: Nina, Oscar, Paula
Clues: MON: Hale dies · WED 9:15 postmark · THU letter arrives
Evidence per suspect: Nina: at sea until Thursday | Oscar: train at 7:00 | Paula: stayed in the village
Solution: Paula
Mechanism (do not reuse): Postmark date after the time of death plus post-office opening hours versus a train alibi.
Script (EN): One of these three mailed a letter for a dead man. And the calendar gives them away. Monday night, old Mr. Hale dies in his study. On Thursday, his lawyer receives a letter, signed by Hale, leaving everything to his niece. The postmark: Wednesday, nine fifteen, the village post office. Nina, the niece: "I was on a cruise until Thursday." Oscar, the neighbor: "On Wednesday I took the seven o'clock train to the city." Paula, the nurse: "I stayed in the village all week." The post office opens at nine. Who mailed the dead man's letter? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Paula. Hale was already dead on Wednesday, so someone mailed it for him. Nina was at sea. Oscar was on a train before the post office even opened. Only Paula was in the village at nine fifteen. Did you solve it? Comment your answer. This is ClueLab.
