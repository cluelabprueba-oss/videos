# CL-0105 — The Bare Roof

Clues (board): BANK ROBBER ON THE RUN · HEAVY SNOW ALL NIGHT · 3 CABINS: 'EMPTY, HEAT OFF'

Solution: Lior
Mechanism (do not reuse): Roof heat loss: snow melts off the roof of a heated building, so after a snowfall a bare, dripping roof proves a cabin claimed empty and unheated is occupied.
Script (EN): Three empty cabins. One roof gives the secret away. A bank robber flees into the mountains. Heavy snow falls all night. At dawn, police check three cabins. Each owner swears: my cabin is empty, and the heating has been off for weeks. Jorge's cabin: the roof lies under thick, untouched snow. Kit's cabin: thick snow on the roof, and fresh footprints to the door. "I checked the pipes." Lior's cabin: the roof is bare and dripping, with icicles at the edges. Who is hiding the fugitive? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Lior. Snow only melts off a roof when the house below is warm. After a night of snow, a bare roof means someone is inside, with the heat on.
