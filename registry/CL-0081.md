# CL-0081 — The Honest Odometer
Status: SCRIPTED (IDEA -> VALIDATED -> SCRIPTED, 2026-11-07, registered BEFORE production)
Created: 2026-11-07 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Suspects: Pete, Lucy, Omar
Evidence per suspect: Pete: gym, 5 km, odometer +10 | Lucy: airport, 25 km, odometer +50 | Omar: dentist, 5 km, odometer +80
Clues (board): SHOP ROBBED AT NOON · SHOP IS 40 KM AWAY · ONE CAR, THREE TRIPS
Solution: Omar
Mechanism (do not reuse): Round-trip odometer arithmetic: an 80 km logged trip equals twice the 40 km to the shop, contradicting a 5 km dentist claim.
Script (EN): The odometer never lies. Only the driver does. A jewelry shop forty kilometers from town is robbed at noon. Three coworkers each borrowed the same company car that afternoon. Every trip is logged on the odometer. Pete drove to the gym, five kilometers away. The odometer went up ten. Lucy drove to the airport, twenty-five kilometers away. The odometer went up fifty. Omar drove to the dentist, five kilometers away. The odometer went up eighty. Who drove to the shop? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Omar. A round trip doubles the distance. Pete's ten and Lucy's fifty match their stories. Eighty is exactly twice the forty kilometers to the shop. Omar's dentist was a lie. Did you solve it?
