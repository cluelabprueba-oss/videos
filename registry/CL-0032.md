Clues: 1) "This machine screams at a guilty hand" · 2) Secret: the box is covered in black ink · 3) Dark room: each touches it alone
Solution: TILLY. The innocent trusted it and touched the box. The guilty feared it and only pretended. Clean hands, guilty mind. The machine was fake. The fear was real..
Mechanism (do not reuse): Fake lie-detector box coated in ink: the guilty avoid touching it, so only the guilty have clean hands
Script (EN): The detective's lie detector was a fake. And it still caught the thief. A rare violin is stolen from the concert hall. Three musicians. No evidence. What nobody knows: the box is covered in black ink. The lights come on. Ruth's fingers are black. Sam's fingers are black. Tilly's hands are perfectly clean. Who stole the violin? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Tilly. The innocent trusted the machine and touched it. The guilty was afraid it would scream, and only pretended. Clean hands, guilty mind.
