Clues (board): PAINTING STOLEN, BACK DOOR · CAMERA: A WIG, A LONG COAT · BUTTONS ON THE WEARER'S RIGHT
Solution: Rhys
Mechanism (do not reuse): Clothing convention: men's coats have the buttons on the wearer's right, women's on the left, so a 'woman' in a right-buttoned coat is a man in a wig.
Script (EN): The camera saw a woman. Her coat buttons say otherwise. A painting is stolen from a gallery. The camera catches a figure in a wig and a long coat, slipping out the back. The buttons run down the wearer's right side. Nell, the receptionist: "I own a red coat. I was at the front desk." Opal, the guide: "I'm the tallest woman here. I was in the cloakroom." Rhys, the guard: "I was on my rounds in the east wing, alone." Who wore that coat? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Rhys. Women's coats button on the wearer's left.
