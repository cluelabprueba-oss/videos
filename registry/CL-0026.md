# CL-0026 — Tell It Backwards
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-03 · Category: Psychological trap · Language: English
Scenario: Warehouse fire at midnight; Detective Marsh has three workers retell their evening backwards. Una (soot on sleeve, welder = false lead) and Rob do it easily; Sid freezes and restarts forward from dinner.
Suspects: Una, Rob, Sid
Clues: 1) reverse-order recall; 2) Una and Rob fluent with fresh details; 3) Sid only forward
Solution: Sid. A rehearsed lie runs only forward.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Reverse-order recall exposes a memorised story
