# CL-0052 — The Cherry Alibi

Created: 2026-11-05 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Suspects: Marco, his uncle
Clues: 1) Robbery on a Saturday in November · 2) Photo of Marco picking ripe cherries dated that Saturday · 3) Cherries ripen in early summer; November trees are bare
False lead: The uncle's confirmation
Solution: Marco (his uncle covers for him)
Mechanism (do not reuse): Seasonal produce vs calendar: ripe cherries cannot be photographed in November, so the dated alibi photo is old/forged.
Script (EN): A photo of ripe cherries. The calendar disagrees. A warehouse is robbed one Saturday in November. Marco says he spent the weekend at his uncle's orchard. He shows a photo: Marco, smiling, picking ripe cherries. The date: that very Saturday. His uncle backs him up. Cherry trees ripen in early summer. In November, they stand bare. Is the alibi true? Five seconds. A lie. Cherries cannot be ripe in November. The photo was taken months earlier, and the date was changed. His uncle covered for him. Comment your answer. This is ClueLab.
