# CL-0016 — The Closed Market
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-03 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Scenario: Mr Dunn killed on the 14th at 3 p.m. (a Monday). Hal claims he was at the harbour fish market; Ivy dentist receipt; Jay seen near house 2:40 (false lead).
Suspects: Hal, Ivy, Jay
Clues: 1) the 14th was a Monday (calendar); 2) market sign: open Tue-Sun; 3) Hal's alibi
Solution: Hal. The fish market is closed on Mondays.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Weekday/opening-days contradiction exposes alibi
