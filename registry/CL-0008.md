# CL-0008 — The Word Nobody Said
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-15 (batch run 2026-11-14 UTC) · Category: Psychological trap · Language: English
Setting: Hale & Sons office. Mr. Hale found dead behind a locked door. Silenced pistol, nobody heard a thing.
Suspects:
- RITA (Business partner): Furious, shouting: "I'm not sorry he's gone!"
- OWEN (Accountant): Holds a gun licence. Stays silent.
- MILES (Assistant): Calm: "Who would shoot a man like that?"
Clues:
1) Police said only: "Mr. Hale was found dead"
2) Silenced weapon: nobody heard a shot
3) No one was told HOW he died
Solution: Miles. The police only said Mr. Hale was found dead. Nobody heard a shot, and nobody was told how he died. Miles asked who would 'shoot' him: only the killer could know it was a gun.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Guilty knowledge: suspect names the undisclosed method of death.
