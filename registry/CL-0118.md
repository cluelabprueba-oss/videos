# CL-0118 — The Noon Rainbow

Clues (board): PAINTING STOLEN AT NOON · MIDSUMMER, A SUMMER SHOWER · RAINBOWS NEED A LOW SUN

Solution: Ivan
Mechanism (do not reuse): Rainbow geometry: a rainbow only forms when the sun is low (below about 42 degrees), so a rainbow under a high midsummer noon sun is impossible.
Script (EN): One alibi includes a rainbow. That's exactly what gives it away. Midsummer. At twelve noon, a painting is stolen from the city museum, while a summer shower passes over town. Remember: a rainbow can only appear when the sun is low in the sky. Hana: "At noon I was in a café, watching the rain." Ivan: "At noon I was hiking. The sun was right above me, and I saw a huge rainbow." Juno, who has a museum pass: "At noon I was at my desk. My boss saw me." Who stole the painting? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Ivan. At midsummer noon, the sun is far too high for a rainbow to form. He invented a pretty detail and gave himself away. Hana's story fits the shower. Juno's pass was a false lead.
