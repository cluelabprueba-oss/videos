# CL-0015 — The Silent Guard Dog
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-03 · Category: Who is the killer · Language: English
Scenario: Vineyard cellar robbed at midnight; guard dog Rex (barks at every engine, bites every stranger) stayed silent. Suspects: Tomas (new driver, bitten Monday), Silas (neighbour, truck passed 2 a.m. = false lead), Marta (cook, feeds Rex daily).
Suspects: Tomas, Silas, Marta
Clues: 1) dog silent; 2) dog barks at engines/strangers; 3) who feeds the dog
Solution: Marta. A silent guard dog implies a familiar person; Tomas and Silas are strangers to it.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Silent guard dog = familiar intruder (dog-that-did-not-bark inference)
