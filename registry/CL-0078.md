# CL-0078 — The Euro Before Its Time

Suspects: Dov, Elsa, Finn
Evidence per suspect: Dov: yellowed paper, pesetas | Elsa: crisp new paper, pesetas | Finn: yellowed paper, euros
Clues (board): OLD GARZA DIES · 3 LOANS, MAY 1998 · THE FARM IS THE PRIZE
Solution: Finn
Mechanism (do not reuse): Anachronism: a letter dated 1998 mentions euros, but euro banknotes and coins only existed from 2002 (pesetas in Spain in 1998).
Script (EN): A dead man's loan letter. And one word that did not exist yet. Old Garza dies, and his farm is up for grabs. Three neighbors each show a loan letter signed by Garza, dated May nineteen ninety-eight. Each one says: the farm is mine, as repayment. Dov's letter, yellowed with age: "I lend you five hundred thousand pesetas." Elsa's letter, crisp and white: "I lend you five hundred thousand pesetas." Finn's letter, yellowed with age: "I lend you five thousand euros." One letter is a fake. Who forged it? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Finn. In nineteen ninety-eight, Spain used pesetas. Euro notes and coins did not exist until two thousand two. Elsa's fresh paper was only a false lead. Finn aged his paper, but he forgot his money.
