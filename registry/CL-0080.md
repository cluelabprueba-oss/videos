Clues (board): THREAT NOTE AT THE BANK · SIGNED AND DATED · DATE: 7 / 18
Solution: Ben
Mechanism (do not reuse): Date-format dialect: 7/18 only parses month-first (US convention); day-first writers (Spain, UK) could never write month 18.
Script (EN): A single date on a note gives the writer away. Look at the numbers. A threatening note is found at the bank. It is signed and dated: seven slash eighteen. Three clerks have worked there for years. One of them wrote it. Ana grew up in Madrid. She says she never saw the note. Ben grew up in Boston. He says he was at lunch. Cai grew up in London. He says he was on the phone. Who wrote the note? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Ben. In Madrid and London, the day comes first. Seven slash eighteen would be month eighteen, which does not exist. Only in America do people write the month first. Ben forgot where he learned to write. Did you solve it? Comment your answer.
