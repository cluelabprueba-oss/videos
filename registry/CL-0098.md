# CL-0098 — The Southern Cross
Status: VALIDATED (IDEA -> VALIDATED 2026-11-08; Drive DB doc body not editable via connector, this registry mirrors it)
Created: 2026-11-08 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Suspects: Sid, Tam, Una
Evidence per suspect: Sid: Scotland: the Big Dipper | Tam: Alberta: the Southern Cross | Una: Australia: the Southern Cross
Clues (board): JEWEL HEIST AT MIDNIGHT · 3 STARGAZING PHOTOS · SOUTHERN CROSS: SOUTH ONLY
Solution: Tam
Mechanism (do not reuse): Celestial geography: the Southern Cross is not visible from high northern latitudes, so a stargazing photo of it from Alberta, Canada is fake or from elsewhere.
Script (EN): A photo of the night sky, with the stars in the wrong half of the world. A jewel heist at midnight. Three suspects each send a photo of the night sky from far away. The Big Dipper hangs in the northern sky. The Southern Cross shines only in the south. Sid: "I camped in Scotland. Here's the Big Dipper." Tam: "I camped in Alberta, Canada. Here's the Southern Cross." Una: "I camped in the Australian outback. Here's the Southern Cross." Whose night sky is fake? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Tam. The Southern Cross never rises over Canada. You must go south, near the equator or beyond, to see it. Una's outback sky was real, and Sid's Big Dipper fits Scotland. Did you solve it?
