# CL-0101 — The Light Coin
Status: SCRIPTED (IDEA -> VALIDATED -> SCRIPTED, 2026-11-10; registered before production)
Created: 2026-11-10 · Category: Think like a detective · Language: English
Suspects: Anton, Bea, Cruz
Evidence per suspect: Anton: 1 coin on the scale | Bea: 2 coins on the scale | Cruz: 3 coins on the scale
Clues (board): REAL COIN 10 G · FAKE 9 G · ONE WEIGHING ONLY · 6 COINS WEIGH 58 GRAMS
False lead: Cruz was seen near the vault
Solution: Bea
Mechanism (do not reuse): One-weighing counterfeit trick: take 1, 2 and 3 coins from the three sacks; the number of grams missing from the total equals the sack number holding the lighter fakes.
Script (EN): One scale. One weighing. And it names the thief. Three guards each keep a sack of gold coins. A real coin weighs ten grams. Someone swapped a whole sack for fakes that weigh nine. The inspector has time for one weighing. Cruz was seen near the vault at night. From Anton's sack, she takes one coin. From Bea's sack, she takes two coins. From Cruz's sack, three coins. Six coins together. The scale reads fifty-eight grams. Whose sack holds the fakes? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Bea. Six real coins would weigh sixty grams.
