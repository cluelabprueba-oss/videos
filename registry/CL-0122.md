# CL-0122 — The Past Tense
Status: SCRIPTED (IDEA -> VALIDATED -> SCRIPTED, 2026-11-11; registered before production)
Created: 2026-11-11 · Category: Psychological trap · Language: English
Suspects: Max, Nell, Pierce
Evidence per suspect: Max: "She IS the kindest..." | Nell: "She'll turn up." | Pierce: "Lena WAS brilliant."
Clues (board): LENA MISSING FOR 2 DAYS · NO BODY, NO NOTE · HUSBAND RAISED HER INSURANCE
False lead: The husband raised her life insurance
Solution: Pierce
Mechanism (do not reuse): Verb-tense slip: while a person is only missing, everyone speaks of her in the present tense; the one who knows she is dead slips into the past tense.
Script (EN): She's only missing. So why does one of them talk about her as if she's gone? Lena Ross has been missing for two days. No body. No note. Her husband recently raised her life insurance. The detective asks three people about her. Max, her husband: "She is the kindest person I know. Please, bring her home." Nell, her sister: "She's stubborn. She'll turn up. She always does." Pierce, her business partner: "Lena was brilliant. She was always the first in the office." Who knows what happened to Lena? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Pierce. Everyone else still speaks about Lena in the present tense. Only someone who knows she is dead slips into the past. The life insurance was a false lead. Did you solve it? Comment your answer.
