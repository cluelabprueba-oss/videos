Script (EN): Everyone drank the same punch. Only one man died. How? A ski lodge party. Everyone drinks punch from the same bowl. The guests gulp theirs and rush out to the slopes. Old Mr. Hollis sips his slowly by the fire for an hour. Then he collapses. Poisoned. Bianca, the host: "I poured every glass, and I drank one myself." Colm, the neighbor: "I only brought the ice, from my own freezer." Dario, the nephew: "I sat beside him all evening. I inherit, yes." Who poisoned the drink? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Colm. The poison was frozen inside his ice cubes. Fast drinkers finished before the ice melted. Hollis sipped for an hour, and the melting ice set the poison free.
