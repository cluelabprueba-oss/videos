Suspects: Three doors
Clues: 1) Three doors, one thief · 2) You pick door one · 3) The guard, who knows, opens empty door three
False lead: The intuition that it is fifty-fifty
Solution: Switch to door two (2 in 3)
Mechanism (do not reuse): Probability (Monty Hall): after a host who knows reveals an empty door, switching wins with probability 2/3.
Script (EN): A thief hides behind one of three doors. Think carefully. You choose door one. The guard, who knows where the thief is, opens door three. Empty. Now he offers a deal: stay with door one, or switch to door two. Most people stay. It feels like fifty-fifty. Stay or switch? Five seconds. Switch. Your first pick had a one in three chance. That never changed. So the other door holds two in three. Switching doubles your odds. Comment your answer. This is ClueLab.
