Question: How did the attacker escape a room bolted from the inside?
Clues: 1) Blood drop in the hallway, outside the door | 2) Professor's own bloody fingerprints on the inside bolt | 3) Blood trail from the door to her desk
False lead: A spare key is missing from the porter's desk.
Solution: The attacker was never inside: the professor was stabbed in the hallway and bolted the door herself.
Explanation: Blood drop outside in the hallway, the professor's own bloody prints on the inner bolt and a trail from door to desk show she was attacked in the hallway, locked herself in to keep the attacker out and collapsed. The missing spare key is a false lead: a bolt cannot be worked with a key.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Locked-room inversion: the victim locked the room herself after being attacked outside.
