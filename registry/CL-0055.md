Solution: The right room (opposite of the pointed room)
Mechanism (do not reuse): Classic one-question liar/truth-teller logic: ask what the other guard would say and go the opposite way.
Script (EN): One question. Two guards. One of them always lies. A thief hides in one of two rooms. Two guards. One always tells the truth, one always lies. You do not know which. The honest guard reports the liar's lie. The liar lies about the honest answer. Either way, the pointed room is wrong. Comment your answer. This is ClueLab.
