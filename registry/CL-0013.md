Question: Who took the brooch?
Clues: 1) Two 20 cm candles lit at 8 PM, burning 2 cm per hour | 2) Henri says he blew one out at 9:30 and left | 3) The blown-out candle measures 14 cm
False lead: Gaspard admired the brooch all evening.
Solution: Henri, the butler
Explanation: Candles 20 cm tall, lit at 8, burn 2 cm per hour. The blown-out candle measures 14 cm: it lost 6 cm, so it burned 3 hours and was put out at 11 PM, not at 9:30 as Henri claims (it would measure 17 cm). Henri was in the room at 11, alone with the brooch. Gaspard admiring the brooch is the false lead.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Candle burn rate used as a clock to time when it was extinguished.

## Narration script

Two candles, lit at the same moment. One of them is a clock.
Moreau villa. At eight, two new candles are lit: twenty centimetres tall, burning two centimetres per hour.
At ten, everyone leaves the dining room. At midnight, Madame's diamond brooch is gone, and one candle has been blown out.
Gaspard, the guest, admired the brooch all evening.
Henri, the butler, says he blew it out at half past nine, then left.
The blown out candle now measures fourteen centimetres.
Who took the brooch? You have five seconds.
Henri. Two centimetres per hour. The candle lost six, so it burned for three hours, from eight to eleven.
Blown out at half past nine, it would measure seventeen. Henri was in that room at eleven, alone with the brooch. Gaspard only admired it.
