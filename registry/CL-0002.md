# CL-0002 — The Mirror Witness
Status: VALIDATED (registered before production; Drive DB Doc body not editable via connector — mirror into Master Mystery Database manually)
Created: 2026-10-31 · Category: Hidden clue / Think like a detective · Language: English
Scenario: Gallery diamond theft; sole witness saw the thief only in a hallway mirror and said the gold ring was on his LEFT hand.
Suspects: Dante (curator, ring on left hand — false lead), Victor (guard, ring on right hand), Iris (assistant, no rings, holds keys).
Clues: 1) witness saw thief only in a mirror; 2) she says ring on LEFT hand; 3) suspects' ring hands.
Solution: Victor. A mirror flips left/right, so the ring was really on the right hand.
Mechanism (do not reuse): mirror left-right reversal of eyewitness testimony.
