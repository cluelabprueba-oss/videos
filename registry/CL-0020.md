# CL-0020 — Three Lies, One Leak
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-03 · Category: Psychological trap · Language: English
Scenario: Product-launch leak to rival. Detective Cole gives each suspect a different fake launch date (Dora 3rd, Emil 9th, Fay 17th). Rival announces launch on the 9th. Dora seen at lunch with rival director (false lead).
Suspects: Dora, Emil, Fay
Clues: 1) three unique fake dates; 2) rival acts on the 9th; 3) Dora's lunch
Solution: Emil. Only his unique fake date came back.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Canary trap: unique false information identifies the leaker
