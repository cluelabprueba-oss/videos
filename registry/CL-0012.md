# CL-0012 — Fire in the Locked Cabin
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-15 (batch run 2026-11-14 UTC) · Category: Impossible crime · Language: English
Setting: Lakeside cabin. Locked all day. Fire broke out at 2 PM on a bright, cloudless day. Heavily insured.
Suspects:
- OLIVER (Owner): In town at 2 PM, with witnesses. Moved his desk to the sunny window yesterday.
- TESS (Electrician): Rewired the cabin that morning. Locked up at 11 AM.
- HAL (Neighbor): Wanted to buy the land. Was fishing on the lake all day.
Clues:
1) The wiring was intact: not electrical
2) On the desk: a round glass water carafe in full sun
3) Scorch mark on the papers right behind the carafe
Solution: The carafe acted as a magnifying glass. Sunlight through the round, water-filled glass focused onto the papers and ignited them at 2 PM. Oliver set it up: he moved the desk into the sun the day before and gave himself a perfect alibi.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Water-filled glass carafe focusing sunlight as a lens (delayed ignition).
