Question: Who poisoned Simon, and how, if nobody entered the room?
Clues: 1) Guard: nobody entered; tea tested clean | 2) Brand-new stationery box was a gift from Clara | 3) A freshly sealed reply envelope, flap still damp
False lead: Nephew Owen argued with Simon; the cook made his tea.
Solution: Clara, the agent — poison on the envelope glue of the gifted stationery.
Explanation: Nobody entered and the tea was clean, so the poison had to be on something Simon used alone: the brand-new stationery. He sealed his reply by licking the envelope flap (still damp). The gift came from Clara. Owen's argument and the cook's tea are false leads.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Poison applied to envelope gum, delivered via gifted stationery into a guarded room.
