# CL-0087 — The Intact Web

Suspects: Dora, Emil, Fritz
Evidence per suspect: Dora: holds the front door key | Emil: muddy boots from the garden | Fritz: saw the burglar himself
Clues (board): SILVER CLOCK STOLEN, 3 AM · CELLAR DOOR LOCKED ALL WEEK · A WEB ACROSS THE FRAME
Solution: Fritz
Mechanism (do not reuse): Absence of disturbance: an unbroken spider web across a cellar door proves nobody used it, so the neighbour who claims to have seen an intruder enter there invented him.
Script (EN): A burglar came through the cellar door. Or so one man says. A spider disagrees. Marsh Cottage, three in the morning. A silver clock vanishes. The cellar door has been locked all week. Across its frame, from side to side, hangs a spider's web. Dora, the cleaner: "I have the front door key. I was asleep at home." Emil, the gardener: "My boots are muddy from the garden. That's all." Fritz, the neighbor: "I saw it from my window. A man opened the cellar door and slipped inside." Who invented the burglar? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Fritz. Anyone opening that door would have torn the web. It was perfectly whole. Nobody used that door. Dora's key and Emil's boots were false leads. Only the thief needed an invented burglar.
