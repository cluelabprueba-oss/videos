Mechanism (do not reuse): Identical twin explains an impossible double presence; scar and family photo are the discriminating clues

One has a scar. Who robbed the bank? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Marc. He is Lucas's identical twin, the scar is his, and he had no alibi.
