# CL-0091 — The Wrong Sunrise

Clues (board): MANSION ROBBED AT DAWN · 3 SUNRISE VIDEOS AT SEA · THE SUN RISES IN THE EAST
Solution: Nat
Mechanism (do not reuse): Cardinal geography: the sun rises in the east, so a sunrise rising out of the sea cannot be filmed on a west-facing coast like Santa Monica.
Script (EN): One sunrise video cannot be real. The sun rose on the wrong side. A mansion is robbed at dawn. Three suspects say they were on faraway beaches, filming the sun rise out of the sea. Remember: the sun always rises in the east. Nat: "I was at Santa Monica, on the west coast of California." Olive: "I was at Miami Beach, on the east coast of Florida." Pat: "I was at Bondi Beach, on the east coast of Australia." Whose sunrise cannot be real? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Nat. On California's west coast, the sea lies to the west. The sun rises behind the land, never out of the water. Olive and Pat faced east, so their sunrises over the sea were possible. Did you solve it?
