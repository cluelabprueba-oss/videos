# CL-0014 — The Wrong Time
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-15 (batch run 2026-11-14 UTC) · Category: Psychological trap · Language: English
Setting: Halden Museum. A gold medal stolen at night. The alarm log has never been made public.
Suspects:
- GRETA (Night guard): Nervous, sweating: "I was on my rounds at 3."
- SIMON (Curator): Calm: "At 3? But it rang at 2:40, didn't it?"
- LOU (Cleaner): Has a museum key. "I was home asleep."
Clues:
1) The real alarm time is secret: 2:40 AM
2) The detective deliberately says "3:00"
3) Only the person who was there knows the real time
Solution: Simon. The detective gave a false time, 3:00, on purpose. The real time, 2:40, was never made public. Simon corrected him: only someone who was there when the alarm went off could know. Greta's nerves were a false lead.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Detective plants a false detail; culprit reflexively corrects it with secret true detail.
