# CL-0014 — The Planted Colour

Question: Who was driving?
Clues: 1) Witness told police: dark blue car (colour never released) | 2) Inspector deliberately says 'a red car' | 3) Andrés corrects to 'dark blue'; his black car is fresh from the body shop
False lead: Pablo owns a red car and panics.
Solution: Andrés
Explanation: The witness said the car was dark blue, but the police never released the colour. Inspector Vega deliberately said 'red'. Andrés corrected him with 'dark blue' — only the driver could know. His black car was just repainted at the body shop. Pablo, who owns a red car, simply panicked (false lead).
Mechanism (do not reuse): Detective plants a false detail; the guilty suspect corrects it with unreleased true knowledge.

## Narration script

The detective lied on purpose. And the guilty man couldn't resist correcting him.
A hit and run on Elm Street, at midnight. The only witness saw the car, not the driver: dark blue. That detail was never made public.
Inspector Vega visits three neighbours, and tells each one: we know it was a red car.
Pablo, who owns a red car, panics and demands a lawyer.
Lucía shrugs: Red? Then it wasn't me. Mine is grey.
Andrés, whose black car is fresh from the body shop, frowns: Red? No. It was dark blue.
Who was driving? You have five seconds.
Andrés. The colour was never released, and Vega said red on purpose.
Only someone who saw that car, or drove it, would know. That fresh black paint hides the blue. Pablo just panicked.
