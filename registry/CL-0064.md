Evidence per suspect: Pia: no key to the office | Quinn: bangs came instantly | Rex: out at sea, heard wind

Mechanism (do not reuse): Speed of sound versus light: bangs heard instantly cannot come from 3 km away.
Script (EN): He watched the fireworks from three kilometers away. But the bangs came too soon. At midnight, fireworks launch from the harbor, and someone breaks into the harbor office. Sound takes three seconds to travel one kilometer. Pia, the guard, stood on the harbor wall, with no key to the office. Quinn, the baker: "I watched from the hilltop, three kilometers away. Each bang came the instant the lights burst." Rex, the sailor: "I was far out at sea. I heard only wind." Who is lying? Five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Quinn.
