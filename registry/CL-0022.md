# CL-0022 — The Tide Table
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-03 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Scenario: Gull Island, man shot at 6 p.m. at close range; island reached only by sandbar at low tide; high tide 5:30-7:30. Nora says she crossed at 6 and found body; Pete pier witnesses; Quinn mainland with rifle (false lead).
Suspects: Nora, Pete, Quinn
Clues: 1) high tide 5:30-7:30; 2) sandbar covered; 3) Nora's claim
Solution: Nora. She could not have walked across; she was already on the island.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Tide table contradicts claimed route
