Clues (board): BARN BURNED AT MIDNIGHT · GASOLINE SMELL: STRONG · 3 RECEIPTS, ONE STATION
Solution: Mo
Mechanism (do not reuse): Capacity arithmetic: a 35-litre tank cannot take a 60-litre purchase, so the surplus went into a can for the arson.
Script (EN): Everybody fills a tank. Only one person filled something else. A barn burns down at midnight. The fire marshal smells gasoline, a lot of it. Three neighbors show a fuel receipt from that evening, all from the same station. Kay drives a family car with a forty-five liter tank. Her receipt: forty liters. Len rides a motorbike with a fifteen liter tank. His receipt: fourteen liters. Mo drives a small hatchback with a thirty-five liter tank. His receipt: sixty liters. Who bought fuel for the fire? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Mo. A thirty-five liter tank cannot hold sixty liters. Twenty-five liters went into a can. Kay and Len bought less than their tanks hold.
