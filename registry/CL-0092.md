# CL-0092 — The Pearl Test

Created: 2026-11-08 · Category: Think like a detective · Language: English
Suspects: Qin, Ruth, Sam
Evidence per suspect: Qin: heaviest, feels smooth | Ruth: shiniest, feels smooth | Sam: dullest, feels gritty
Clues (board): DUCHESS'S PEARLS STOLEN · 3 NECKLACES, 2 ARE GLASS · TEST: ROLL ON THE TEETH
Solution: Sam
Mechanism (do not reuse): Material test: real pearls feel gritty when rubbed on the teeth, glass or plastic imitations feel smooth; weight and shine can be faked.
Script (EN): Two necklaces are glass. One is the stolen pearls. The test uses your teeth. The Duchess's pearls vanish from the museum. Three curators each keep a necklace at home. Two are glass copies. One is the real thing. Qin's necklace is the heaviest. Rubbed on a tooth, it feels perfectly smooth. Ruth's necklace is the shiniest. Rubbed on a tooth, it feels perfectly smooth. Sam's necklace is dull and plain. Rubbed on a tooth, it feels gritty, like fine sand. Whose pearls are real? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Sam. Real pearls feel gritty against the teeth. Glass and plastic are smooth. Weight and shine can be faked, and Qin and Ruth's glass proved it. Did you solve it?
