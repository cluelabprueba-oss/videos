# CL-0094 — The Egg That Floated
Status: VALIDATED (IDEA -> VALIDATED 2026-11-08; Drive DB doc body not editable via connector, this registry mirrors it)
Created: 2026-11-08 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Suspects: Abel, Cass, Dev
Evidence per suspect: Abel: egg sinks, lies flat | Cass: egg sinks, lies flat | Dev: egg floats to the top
Clues (board): FARMER DEAD AT DAWN · 3 BASKETS OF FRESH EGGS · TEST: A BOWL OF WATER
Solution: Dev
Mechanism (do not reuse): Egg freshness: a fresh egg sinks and lies flat, an old egg takes in air and floats, so eggs claimed as gathered at dawn but floating are weeks old.
Script (EN): Three baskets of eggs. One egg floats. That is the liar. The farmer is found dead in the barn at dawn. Three workers say they spent that dawn in the henhouse, gathering eggs. The inspector drops one egg from each basket into a bowl of water. Abel: "I gathered mine at sunrise." His egg sinks, and lies flat. Cass: "I gathered mine at first light." Her egg sinks, and lies flat. Dev: "I filled my basket before anyone was up." His egg floats to the top. Who lied about the henhouse? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Dev. A fresh egg is heavy and sinks. As an egg ages, air seeps through the shell, and it floats. Dev's eggs were weeks old. He never gathered them that dawn. Did you solve it? Comment your answer.
