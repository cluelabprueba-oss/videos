# CL-0108 — The Answer That Wasn't
Status: SCRIPTED (IDEA -> VALIDATED -> SCRIPTED, 2026-11-10; registered before production)
Created: 2026-11-10 · Category: Psychological trap · Language: English
Suspects: Rosa, Saul, Tobias
Evidence per suspect: Rosa: "No. I didn't take it." | Saul: "No. Search my bag." | Tobias: "Why would I do that?"
Clues (board): DIAMOND RING GONE, SUITE 9 · ROSA CLEANED THE SUITE · ONE QUESTION: DID YOU TAKE IT?
False lead: Saul is nervous; Rosa cleaned the suite
Solution: Tobias
Mechanism (do not reuse): Non-denial denial: asked a direct yes-or-no question, the guilty one never says no, deflecting with a question and his reputation instead.
Script (EN): Three answers to one simple question. Listen closely. A diamond ring vanishes from suite nine of a grand hotel. Rosa cleaned the suite that morning. The detective asks each staff member one simple question: did you take the ring? Rosa: "No. I did not take it." Saul, shaking: "No. I didn't. Search my bag if you want." Tobias: "Me? I've worked here twenty years. Why would I ever do something like that?" Who took the ring? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Tobias. Listen again. He never said no. He answered with a question, and with his reputation. Rosa and Saul denied it plainly. Saul's nerves and Rosa's cleaning were false leads. Did you solve it? Comment your answer. This is ClueLab.
