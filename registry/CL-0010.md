Question: Who is lying?
Clues: 1) Necklace stolen 7:40 PM; sunset 7:42 PM | 2) Hotel map: west wing faces the sea (sunset), east wing faces the mountains | 3) Julia (room 31, east wing) says she watched the sun sink from her balcony
False lead: Ana, the receptionist, left her desk at 7:30.
Solution: Julia, room 31
Explanation: Julia's room is in the east wing, facing the mountains. The sun sets in the west, over the sea, so she could not have watched it sink from her balcony. She invented the view to cover being at the safe at 7:40. Ana's absence is a camera-verified break (false lead).
Mechanism (do not reuse): Alibi describes a sunset from a balcony that faces east.
