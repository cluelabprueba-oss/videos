Script (EN): A threatening note, and two letters are in the wrong place. A threat arrives, typed on a public library computer: "Zou have until Friday, or zou pay." On German keyboards, the Y and Z keys sit in different places from American ones. Alma's home keyboard is German. Ben's home keyboard is French, and he was fired last month. Cora's home keyboard is American, and she was seen leaving late. Who typed the threat? Five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Alma. A German typist's fingers hit the wrong key for Y on an American keyboard, and it types Z. Ben's French habit would swap A and Q instead, and Cora types the way the library does. Did you solve it?
