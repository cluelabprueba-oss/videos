# CL-0114 — The Poisoned Envelope
Status: SCRIPTED (IDEA -> VALIDATED -> SCRIPTED, 2026-11-11; registered before production)
Created: 2026-11-11 · Category: Impossible crime · Language: English
Suspects: Ruth, Simon, Tara
Evidence per suspect: Ruth: cooked dinner, made tea | Simon: mailed a return envelope | Tara: emailed her thesis
Clues (board): DIED ALONE, DOOR LOCKED · POISON ON HIS TONGUE ONLY · FOOD AND TEA: CLEAN
False lead: Ruth cooked his dinner and made his tea
Solution: Simon
Mechanism (do not reuse): Poison on envelope glue: a prepaid return envelope with poisoned gum kills the recipient when he licks it shut, so the killer never comes near the locked room.
Script (EN): He died alone, behind a locked door. The killer was miles away. Professor Lund is found dead at his desk, the door locked. Poison on his tongue, but his food and tea are clean. Beside him, one envelope, freshly sealed. Ruth, the housekeeper, cooked his dinner and made his tea. Simon, his business partner, mailed him a contract with a return envelope: "Sign it and send it back today." Tara, his student, emailed him her thesis that morning. Who poisoned the professor? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Simon. The poison was on the glue of his return envelope. When Lund licked it to seal it, he poisoned himself. Simon never came near him.
