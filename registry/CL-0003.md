# CL-0003 — The Snow Clock
Status: VALIDATED
Status history: IDEA 2026-11-15 → VALIDATED 2026-11-15
Created: 2026-11-15 (UTC) · Category: Who is the killer · Language: English
Question: Who was in the lodge at eleven and lied about it?
Clues: 1) Snow fell from 10 PM to 2 AM; murder at 11 PM | 2) Nico claims he arrived at 2 AM | 3) Nico's car under 10 cm of snow, same as Tomas's (parked all day); Lena's car (1:30 AM) only a dusting
False lead: Tomas argued with the victim at dinner.
Solution: Nico, the driver
Explanation: Snow fell 10 PM-2 AM. A car arriving at 2 AM would have a bare roof; Nico's was buried as deep as a car parked since 10 PM, so he was already there at 11 PM. Lena's dusting fits 1:30 AM. Tomas's argument is the false lead.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Snow depth on car roofs dates each arrival during a known snowfall window.

## Narration script

Three cars. One killer. And the snow remembers when each one arrived.
Snow fell on the Varga lodge from ten until two in the morning. At eleven, Mr. Varga was killed.
Tomas, his partner, argued with him at dinner, and never left the lodge.
Lena, his niece, says she arrived at one thirty, and found the body.
Nico, the driver, swears he only drove up at two.
Police check the cars. Tomas's: under ten centimetres of snow.
Lena's: barely a dusting.
Nico's: buried just as deep.
Who lied? You have five seconds.
It was Nico. The snow stopped at two. A car arriving at two would have a bare roof.
Nico's car was buried as deep as one parked since ten. He was already there when Varga died. The argument was just noise.
