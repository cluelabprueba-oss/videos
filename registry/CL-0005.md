Question: Who killed Hugo?
Clues: 1) Broken watch frozen at 9:15 | 2) Boarding pass London→Madrid, landed that evening | 3) Alibi gaps: Felipe 9:00-9:30, Carmen 10:00-10:30
False lead: Felipe has no alibi from 9:00 to 9:30.
Solution: Carmen, the ex-wife
Explanation: Hugo had just landed from London and never reset his watch. London is one hour behind Madrid, so 9:15 on the broken watch was 10:15 in Madrid, inside Carmen's unaccounted hour (10:00-10:30). Felipe's 9:00-9:30 gap is the false lead.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Broken watch still set to another time zone shifts the true time of death.
