# CL-0005 — Glass on the Lawn
Status: VALIDATED
Created: 2026-11-15 (batch run 2026-11-14 UTC) · Category: Hidden clue · Language: English
Setting: Grant house. Jewelry stolen overnight. Insured for half a million.
Suspects:
- MR. GRANT (Owner): "A thief smashed the back window from the garden." Jewelry insured last month.
- DEX (Gardener): Muddy boots. Works in the garden every day.
- MRS. PIKE (Neighbor): Heard glass breaking at 2 AM. Saw no one.
Clues:
1) Muddy footprints lead from the window inside
2) Almost all the broken glass lies OUTSIDE, on the lawn
3) Jewelry insured one month ago
Solution: The glass. A window smashed from the garden scatters glass INSIDE the house. Here the shards fell on the lawn: it was broken from inside. Mr. Grant staged the burglary for the insurance. The muddy prints were planted.
Mechanism (do not reuse): Direction of broken glass reveals window was broken from inside (staged burglary).
