# CL-0096 — The Falling Horn
Status: VALIDATED (IDEA -> VALIDATED 2026-11-08; Drive DB doc body not editable via connector, this registry mirrors it)
Created: 2026-11-08 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Suspects: Kira, Lou, Mace
Evidence per suspect: Kira: horn got lower | Lou: horn got higher | Mace: horn got lower, then faded
Clues (board): BANK ROBBED AT MIDNIGHT · GETAWAY CAR, HORN BLARING · 3 WITNESSES ON PORCHES
Solution: Lou
Mechanism (do not reuse): Doppler effect: a sound source moving away drops in pitch, so a witness describing a rising horn as the car left was never on the porch.
Script (EN): A horn rising in pitch as the car drove away. That is impossible. Midnight. A getaway car races away from the bank, horn blaring. Three neighbors say they stood on their porches and watched it drive away from them. Kira: "The horn sounded lower and lower as it left." Lou: "The horn rose higher and higher as it sped away." Mace: "Loud at first, then lower, then quiet, as it left." Who was never on that porch? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Lou. When a sound moves away from you, its pitch drops. It only rises while it comes toward you. That is the Doppler effect. Lou described something he never heard. Did you solve it? Comment your answer.
