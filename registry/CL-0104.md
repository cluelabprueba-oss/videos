Mechanism (do not reuse): Wind naming convention: a west wind blows from the west, so smoke drifts east; a house west of the fire stays clear.
Script (EN): The wind decides where smoke goes. One suspect forgot. A barn burns at midnight. The weather station logs a strong west wind all night. A west wind blows from the west, toward the east. Ivo lives west of the barn: "The smoke rolled right over my house. I could barely breathe." Who was not at home? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Ivo. A west wind carried the smoke east, away from his house. His home stayed clear, just like Hal's. Ivo breathed that smoke at the barn itself. Gwen's story fits the wind perfectly. Did you solve it?
