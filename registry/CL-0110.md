Every home on the street has a smart meter that logs electricity every thirty minutes. The detective prints three graphs. Yuri: "I was home, reading by one lamp." His graph: low, but steady. Zoe: "I was out at the cinema." Her graph: low and flat. Abe: "I was home. I cooked in the oven, ran the washing machine, watched TV." His graph: low and flat. Not a single rise. Who was not at home? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Abe. An oven, a washing machine and a TV would send his graph soaring. It never moved. Nobody was home.
