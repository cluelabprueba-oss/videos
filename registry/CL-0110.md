# CL-0110 — The Flat Line
Status: SCRIPTED (IDEA -> VALIDATED -> SCRIPTED, 2026-11-10; registered before production)
Created: 2026-11-10 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Suspects: Yuri, Zoe, Abe
Evidence per suspect: Yuri: home: one reading lamp | Zoe: out at the cinema | Abe: home: oven, washer, TV
Clues (board): BURGLARY ACROSS TOWN, 7-9 PM · SMART METERS LOG EVERY 30 MIN · 3 HOMES, 3 POWER GRAPHS
False lead: Zoe's empty house
Solution: Abe
Mechanism (do not reuse): Smart-meter log: a flat zero electricity reading contradicts a claim of running the oven, washing machine and TV at home.
Script (EN): Three alibis. Three power graphs. One of them is a lie. A house across town is burgled between seven and nine. Every home on the street has a smart meter that logs electricity every thirty minutes. The detective prints three graphs. Yuri: "I was home, reading by one lamp." His graph: low, but steady. Zoe: "I was out at the cinema." Her graph: almost zero. Abe: "I was home. I cooked in the oven, ran the washing machine, watched TV." His graph: flat at zero. Who was not at home? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Abe. An oven, a washing machine and a TV would send his graph soaring. It never moved. Nobody was home. Zoe's near-zero fits a night out, and Yuri's lamp fits his small, steady line. Did you solve it? Comment your answer. This is ClueLab.
