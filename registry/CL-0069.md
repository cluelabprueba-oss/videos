# CL-0069 — The Wrong Side of the Road
Status: SCRIPTED (registered 2026-11-06 before production; PRODUCED -> GITHUB_UPLOADED -> METRICOOL_IMPORTED -> SCHEDULED to follow)
Created: 2026-11-06 · Category: Spot the lie · Language: English
Suspects: Kai, Lia, Moe
Clues: noon: shop robbed · London traffic: LEFT · 3 alibi photos
Evidence per suspect: Kai: bus on the left | Lia: taxi on the left | Moe: cars on the right
Solution: Moe
Mechanism (do not reuse): Driving side by country: photo of right-hand traffic cannot be London (left-hand traffic).
Script (EN): Three alibi photos, all said to be taken in London. But one street is on the wrong side. A watch shop in London is robbed at noon. Each suspect sends a photo from the city. In Britain, cars drive on the left. Kai sends a red bus, passing on the left. He owes the shop money. Lia sends a black taxi on the left, beside a clock tower. Moe sends a busy street, with every car driving on the right. Whose photo was not taken in London? Five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Moe. British traffic keeps left. His street drives on the right, so the photo was taken abroad. Kai owed money, but his photo checks out. Did you solve it? Comment your answer. This is ClueLab.
