Question: Who is lying?
Clues: 1) Heavy rain from 8:00 to 9:30; theft at 8:15 | 2) Rita says she walked 20 minutes from the bus without an umbrella, arriving at 9 | 3) Rita's coat and hair are bone dry
False lead: Paul is soaked and muddy.
Solution: Rita, the cousin
Explanation: Rain fell 8:00-9:30. A 20-minute walk without an umbrella during it would soak her; her dry coat and hair mean she was already inside before 8, when the jewellery box was emptied (8:15). Paul's mud is from walking his dog in the rain (false lead); Owen came by taxi after the rain.
Mechanism (do not reuse): A person claiming to have walked through a timed downpour is completely dry.

## Narration script

It poured for ninety minutes. One of these three says she walked through it, and stayed perfectly dry.
Heavy rain fell from eight until half past nine. At eight fifteen, someone emptied Mrs. Hart's jewellery box upstairs.
Paul, the neighbour, arrived at nine with his dog. Both are soaked and muddy.
Rita, the cousin, says she walked twenty minutes from the bus, no umbrella, arriving at nine. Her coat and hair: bone dry.
Owen, the nephew, came by taxi at ten, after the rain. Dry too.
Who is lying? You have five seconds.
Rita. Twenty minutes in that storm, without an umbrella, would have soaked her to the bone.
Her coat is dry because she was already inside before eight, upstairs, at the jewellery box. The muddy neighbour? He just walked his dog.
