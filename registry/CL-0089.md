# CL-0089 — The Daffodil Photo

Suspects: Gus, Ida, Jon
Evidence per suspect: Gus: red leaves on the ground | Ida: bare trees, holly berries | Jon: field of yellow daffodils
Clues (board): SHOP ROBBED: NOVEMBER 3 · LONDON, AFTERNOON · 3 SELFIES, ONE DATE
Solution: Jon
Mechanism (do not reuse): Seasonal botany: daffodils bloom in March-April, so a selfie 'dated 3 November' in London showing daffodils in full bloom is months old with an edited date stamp.
Script (EN): Three selfies, all dated the same day. One of them is from another season. A jewelry shop in London is robbed on the third of November. Each suspect shows a park selfie, stamped that very day. Gus: "Look at my photo. Red leaves are all over the ground." Ida: "Look at mine. Bare trees, and bright holly berries." Jon: "Look at mine. A field of yellow daffodils in full bloom." Whose photo is not from that day? You have five seconds. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. It was Jon. Daffodils bloom in spring, in March or April, never in November. His photo was months old, and he edited the date stamp. Red leaves and holly berries belong to autumn. Did you solve it?
